⌨️ Shortcut Keyboard:

  • Space - Play/Pause
  • R - Reset
  • A - Akhir
  • ← - Prev Step
  • → - Next Step
  • +/- - Zoom In/Out
  • 0 - Reset Zoom
  • H - Reset View
  • ↑↓←→ - Pan
  • T - Toggle Titik
  • S - Screenshot
  • D - Dark Mode
